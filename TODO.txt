    Verified, and it already worked (localStorage is disk-backed, not

3) Why template? Why extdata? What do they do? Maybe delete them?

    Both load-bearing, don't delete. templates/tutorial.qmd is the starter
    file create_tutorial() copies and fills in ({{title}}) -- delete it and
    create_tutorial() breaks. extdata/ holds two things: _extensions/r-wasm/
    live/ is the vendored quarto-live extension that add_live_extension()
    copies into every tutorial, which is what makes format: live-html
    render at all; extdata/quiz/ is our own quiz.js/quiz.css -- the runtime
    behind question(), quiz(), student_info(), download_answers_button().
    Delete either and rendering, or every interactive widget, silently
    stops working. The extdata/templates names are just conventional R
    package folder names (extdata = bundled external assets, used all over
    CRAN; templates = starting-point files to copy), not something R
    enforces -- purely for readability.
